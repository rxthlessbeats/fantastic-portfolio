## Introduction

Continuous **arterial blood pressure (ABP)** monitoring is invasive and impractical for routine use. This project trains **non-invasive surrogates**—**ECG** (lead II) and **photoplethysmography (PPG)**—to estimate **systolic (SBP)** and **diastolic (DBP)** blood pressure using the **[MIMIC Waveform Database](https://physionet.org/content/mimic3wdb/1.0/)** (MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV waveform subsets).

The core model is a **hybrid CNN–LSTM** (`BP_Model` in PyTorch): convolutional blocks extract local morphology, stacked **bidirectional LSTMs** model temporal context, and a linear head regresses mmHg labels. Predictions are evaluated with the **[British Hypertension Society (BHS) protocol](https://www.bhsoc.org/)**—reporting the percentage of estimates within **5, 10, and 15 mmHg** of reference ABP—and assigned letter grades **A–D**.

## Problem & data

**Goal:** From a 5-second window of synchronized ECG + PPG, predict the **SBP** and **DBP** derived from the invasive ABP waveform in the same window.

**Signals (per segment):**

- **ECG** — lead II, band-pass filtered (NeuroKit2)
- **PPG** — plethysmography (`Pleth`), filtered
- **ABP** — used only for labels (filtered 0.5–20 Hz)

**Label generation:** Peaks and valleys on ABP are detected and refined (`sleepecg`, local peak correction). Mean pressure at corrected peaks → **SBP**; mean at valleys → **DBP**. Segments failing quality checks (heart-rate agreement, peak ratios, pulse pressure range) are discarded per the selection algorithm in `processing.py`.

**Dataset scale (subject-level split, 80% / 10% / 10%):**

- ~**230k** training windows
- ~**33k** validation
- ~**46k** test  

Splits are by **subject ID** so the same patient never appears in both train and test.

## Pipeline overview

1. **Waveform access** — `wfdb` reads MIMIC-III/IV segments with ECG, PPG, and ABP (`data-exploration.ipynb`, `mimic3_data.ipynb`)
2. **Segmentation** — 5-second non-overlapping windows (`split_segment` in `processing.py`)
3. **Quality filter** — HR consistency, morphology ratios, pulse pressure bounds
4. **Training** — `train_torch.ipynb` on `final_records_mimic3_all_with_id.parquet`
5. **Evaluation** — `evaluation.py` BHS protocol + MAE / RMSE

## Key takeaways

- **Subject-level splits** are critical for realistic generalization in ICU waveform data.
- **DBP** is easier than **SBP** under this setup (lower MAE, tighter BHS margins).
- CNN–LSTM hybrids capture both **beat morphology** (conv) and **short-term dynamics** (LSTM) better than either block alone in this pipeline.
- ABP remains the label source only—deployment targets cuff-free monitoring from wearable ECG/PPG.

## Limitations & future work

**Limitations:** R² on the test split is near zero (high inter-subject variance); models are trained primarily on MIMIC-III exports in the published training notebook; waveform access requires PhysioNet credentials.

**Future work:** End-to-end multi-task SBP+DBP head, calibration per subject, and external validation on wearable datasets beyond ICU waveforms.
