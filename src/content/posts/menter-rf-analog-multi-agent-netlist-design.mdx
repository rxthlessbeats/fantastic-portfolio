## Introduction

**MenTeR** (Multi-agenT workflow for end-to-end RF/Analog circuits **Netlist** design) targets a bottleneck in analog IC development: flows that are slow, expert-driven, and heavy on trial-and-error. The system uses **specialized LLM agents** that collaborate on specification understanding, circuit optimization, testbench generation, and schematic/netlist output—reducing manual iteration while expanding design-space exploration.

Published at **[IEEE ICLAD 2025](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11105933/)** ([arXiv:2505.22990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22990)). Authors include researchers from **MediaTek** and **MediaTek Technology** (Purdue-affiliated).

## Problem

RF/analog design bridges digital systems and physical signals, but netlist and schematic work still depends on deep designer intuition. Prior LLM work often stops at simplified benchmarks or single-block amplifiers; industrial flows need **scalable**, **validated** automation from specs through testbenches to netlists—including multi-block and, eventually, system-level targets (e.g. bandgap references, PLLs).

## Methods

MenTeR is an **end-to-end multi-agent workflow** with interactive checkpoints where engineers can intervene.

### Multi-agent workflow

Three specialized agents divide the design flow:

- **PI agent** — specification and parameter-intent reasoning from documents and user goals.
- **Circuit agent** — topology, sizing, and netlist/schematic generation.
- **Testbench agent** — simulation setup and validation loops before sign-off.

### Diagram-Aware RAG

**DA-RAG** retrieves schematic- and diagram-aware context (not text-only chunks) so LLMs ground decisions in visual circuit structure and domain layouts.

**Chain-of-Stage (CoS)** adds domain-specific staged reasoning (building on chain-of-thought) without large proprietary fine-tuning datasets. Together with DA-RAG, these pieces support scalable flow from single-block designs toward multi-block and system-level targets.

Compared with a single monolithic agent, the multi-agent layout mirrors how analog teams divide roles and keeps failures localized to stages.

## Contributions

- First reported **fully automated multi-agent** framework for **end-to-end RF/analog netlist** generation with LLMs (single-block → multi-block → system-level roadmap).
- **DA-RAG** and self-referential techniques to strengthen testbench writing and cut engineer intervention.
- **CoS agent** combining analog domain knowledge with staged CoT-style reasoning.

Experiments emphasize **real-world analog systems**, faster design cycles, and broader search over topologies and parameters than manual-only flows.

## Key takeaways

- MenTeR frames analog design as a **pipeline of cooperating agents**, not one-shot code generation.
- **DA-RAG** and **CoS** are the main levers for domain fidelity without immediate RLHF/DPO on analog preferences.
- Human-in-the-loop **checkpoints** keep the flow deployable while automation handles repetitive trial-and-error.
- Long-term vision: **RF/Analog copilots** that pair with designers on complex blocks and systems.
